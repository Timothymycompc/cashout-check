# Principles

CashOut Check is its own project, built on the same principles as Tim's other safety tools.

## Why we build

To help people stop losing money to fake casinos and play-and-earn games. Not to add more junk or false data to the
internet, and never to lead anyone astray.

## What a check means

A check doesn't make a casino or game safe, and it can't. It looks for the known ways people lose money there: fake
winnings that can't be withdrawn, payments demanded before a withdrawal, copied licences, throwaway sites, balances
that only exist on your phone. "No known traps found" really means "none of the traps we know about are present."
Every rule exists because it matches a real case we investigated, and each one says which.

## How we build

- **Real data, with its source.** Every fact comes from the site itself, a domain registry, Telegram's public pages, a
  public blockchain or a case file we keep, and says which.
- **"Unknown" beats a guess.** When a site doesn't answer or a source is down, the check says so. It never fills the gap.
- **Every rule has a reason.** A rule exists only if it matches a real way people lost money, written down in plain words.
- **Tested on real cases.** Every rule is tested on recorded real scams and on ordinary, long-running sites, and we
  measure both: scams caught (with and without our own case files) and honest sites wrongly flagged.
- **Plain words.** Results say what was found and what to do, for people who aren't technical.
- **Accusations need evidence.** A site is only called part of a known scam when we hold the evidence file for it.
- **Finish before adding.** New ideas go in LATER.md until the current work is done.
